# LineCatch vision

From an interview with the founder, October 3, 2026. Read this before building anything for LineCatch. It says what we're building toward and why.

## In one line

**LineCatch fills empty chairs, and makes the work look amazing doing it.**

---

## The company

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Founder | Solo founder |
| Founder's time goes to | Building the product, supporting barbers |
| How it started | A tech-first idea: missed-call texting and automation for barbers |
| Stage | Pre-launch |
| How it's built | The founder directs; AI writes the code |

## Who it's for

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| First customers | All barbers: solo barbers, booth renters and shop owners |
| #1 job | **Bring clients back** |
| Price | $20–$40 a month |

## How it should work

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Barber effort | Hands-on while they set it up. After that they mostly check their **schedule** and **revenue**, and edit their **portfolio photos**. Everything else runs on its own. |
| Home screen | **Revenue first**, as it is now |
| The system | Missed calls become booking links. Wednesday reminders, loyalty and review requests bring clients back without the barber lifting a finger. |

**Rule for new features:** if a barber has to remember to do it every day, automate it.

## What we want to be known for

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Known for | **Filling empty chairs**, with **the best visuals** of any tool in the space |
| Visual edge | **Portfolio pages** that look like a custom website, and **photo quality**: layouts, galleries, before/after |
| Competitors | Booksy, Squire, Square, Calendly |
| How we win | Visuals. They all handle booking; our pages make the work look amazing. |
| Not a competitor | Pen, paper and Instagram DMs. LineCatch makes that life easier, so those barbers are our customers. |

## Where it's going

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| 2–3 years | **All beauty pros**, not just barbers |
| After barbers | **Hair stylists, nail techs and lash artists**: tight-knit communities like barbers, so word of mouth spreads fast. **Tattoo artists**: a strong fit for the visuals alone, since they'd buy a page that makes their work look amazing. |
| Premium tier | **Custom design** for select clients: the founder personally designs their page and brand, for more money |

## How we grow

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| Barber referrals | Main engine. A referred barber gets 50% off the first month; the referrer gets a free month. |
| Social media | Show the visuals. The portfolio pages are the content. |
| Partnerships | Barber schools, suppliers, events |
| Door to door with QR stickers | **Launch only.** It gets the first barbers and starts referrals, and it tests whether stickers are worth it. The system works without stickers, and walking into shops doesn't scale. |

## How we'll know it's working

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| Clients come back | Rebookings from Wednesday reminders and win-backs |
| Missed calls get booked | Calls that turned into appointments |
| Revenue goes up | The barber's monthly revenue grows |
| Barbers stay | They keep paying month after month |

## What to build next

| Order | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Automatic phone numbers** | Every barber gets a LineCatch number at signup, with no manual step. Without one, missed-call texts can't go out. |
| 2 | **Billing (Stripe)** | Start charging $20–$40 a month, and apply the referral free months and 50% discounts already being recorded. |
| 3 | **Portfolio redesign** | Our edge. Make every barber's page the best-looking page in the space. |

Also needed before launch: carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) for marketing texts, and the steps in [`LAUNCH.md`](LAUNCH.md).
